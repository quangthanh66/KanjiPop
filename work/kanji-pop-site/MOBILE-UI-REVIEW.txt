Scope: local-only mobile HUD redesign for the cosmic gem sliding-pair game.

   Prioritize the core loop, separate transient feedback from the HUD and put deeper management behind a menu.

   Apply compact contextual guidance and portrait-first layout. Preserve the existing open-ended play-first rules.

Problems addressed:
- Header redundancy and small text competed with the board.
- Variable coaching/feedback changed available board space.
- Four equal-weight actions diluted the primary help action.
- Light card styling conflicted with the cosmic gem board.
- Later levels contain eight targets and require compact progress indicators.

Result:
- Fullscreen navy cosmic play UI; no surrounding phone card.
- Compact header and progress; stable board region; separate message region.
- Thumb dock: Undo, prominent Hint, Menu. Restart/Album/Settings/Help remain accessible in Menu.
- Landscape places progress left and actions right; safe-area insets respected.
- Compact eight-slot progress indicators retain accessible labels.
- Desktop remains 7x5; mobile remains 4x5. No save schema or engine changes.
